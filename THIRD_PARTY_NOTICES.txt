DSH Remote protocol and test fixture

Source: https://github.com/liguobao/ds-harness-remote
Revision: c374397dea2bea0e5a81adc993553be992102bc6

Packages/DSHCore/Tests/DSHCoreTests/Fixtures/noise-ik.json is copied unchanged
from fixtures/crypto/v1/noise-ik.json. Its private keys are public deterministic
test vectors, never device credentials.

Packages/DSHCore/Sources/DSHCore/SecureChannel.swift adapts the SecureMessageCodec
framing from packages/protocol/src/index.ts. The NoiseIK.swift prologue and
fixed-suite behavior follow packages/crypto/src/noise.ts. The Noise state
machine is implemented locally with Apple CryptoKit; no clatterjs source is
included. Test responder calculations are local validation code.
